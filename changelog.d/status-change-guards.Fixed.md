- **A manual status change left the publish schedule armed, update accepted a status on lifecycle types, and rollback could overwrite a concurrent edit.** Changing status away from Scheduled, through change-status, update or collection push, now clears the publish time. Update and collection push refuse a status change on a type that declares a lifecycle with 400, and an update dry run gives the same answer. Rollback answers 409 when the entry changed after it was read.
