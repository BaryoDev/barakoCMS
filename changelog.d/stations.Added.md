- **Station keys.** `POST /api/stations`, `GET /api/stations` and `POST /api/stations/{id}/revoke`, gated by the new `manage_stations` capability. A station is a machine with no signed-in person, such as a kiosk or display board, holding a fixed list of capabilities in one tenant. The key (`bsta_` prefix) is returned once and only its hash is stored. A caller can only grant capabilities they hold, and `*` is refused.
