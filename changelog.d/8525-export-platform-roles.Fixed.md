- **Config export no longer writes platform roles or the rules they hold, so an export applies cleanly to the system it came from and to another one.** The `roles` section holds the tenant's own roles only, the way it already left out the seeded roles, and `permissionRules` leaves out rules on platform roles. Before, a platform role that was also invitable made the apply of that same export fail, and the rules named a role the other install did not have. Apply leaves a platform role that the document does not name alone.
