- **A recompute skips an entry it cannot rewrite.** The entry keeps its value, its id is recorded, and the change ends in the new state `completed_with_skips` instead of locking the type. `POST /api/content-types/field-changes/{id}/retry` recomputes the skipped entries, and `GET /api/content-types/{name}/field-changes` lists a type's changes. The change response gains `skippedCount`, `skipped`, `retryOf` and `retriedBy`. The state, the fields and both routes are part of contract 17, so the contract version does not move. See `docs/field-changes.md`.
