- **Rollup fields.** A field can total the entries of another type that point at it, with `rollup: { from, via, fn, field, round }` and `fn` of `count`, `sum`, `min`, `max` or `avg`. The server keeps the value a moment after each related write commits, and a client that sends one gets a 400. Saving a rollup needs read on the summed and the reference field, and its level must be at least as strict as both. A change to the `from` type that would break a rollup answers 409. Adding or changing a rollup on a type with entries starts a recompute field change.
