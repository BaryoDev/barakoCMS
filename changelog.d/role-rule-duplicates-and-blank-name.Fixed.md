- **A role could be saved with a blank name, or with two rules for one content type.** A create or update now refuses a blank name, and refuses two rules that name the same content type (case and surrounding spaces ignored). An update may still send back a repeat the stored role already holds, but not add another. The config engine also plans and applies over a role that already holds two such rules, where it used to fail, and keeps the first of the two.
