- **The `site` blueprint had no coming soon fields.** barakoPress coming soon mode
  (BaryoDev/barakoPress#35) reads `ComingSoon`, `ComingSoonBlocks` and `PreviewKeyHash` from the
  tenant's `site` entry, and a tenant had to add them to its own type. The blueprint now creates
  them. `PreviewKeyHash` is a Public field, since barakoPress reads the entry anonymously, so the
  preview key must be generated from at least 32 random bytes; `docs/site-settings.md` shows how.
