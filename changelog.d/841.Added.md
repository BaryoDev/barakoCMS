- **A site can be held back, and previewed while it is.** The `site` blueprint now creates `Mode`
  (`Live` or `Holding`, unset means `Live`) and `HoldingPath`, the path of the page a frontend shows
  while holding. They are presentation only; the API hides nothing. Share links let people preview a
  held site: `POST /api/site/share-links` returns a generated key once and stores only its hash,
  `GET` lists links without keys, `DELETE /api/site/share-links/{id}` revokes one, and
  `POST /api/public/site/share-links/redeem` answers 200 with the expiry or 404, rate limited per
  client IP (`RateLimiting:SiteShare`). Links expire after 30 days by default, 90 at most, and are
  audited. Managing them needs update permission on `site`.
