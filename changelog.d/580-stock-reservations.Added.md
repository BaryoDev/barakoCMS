- **Core can reserve against a counter.** `IStockReservations` takes units from a counter in one statement that holds the counter's row, so two callers racing for the last unit get one success and one refusal, and the balance never crosses the counter's floor. It runs in the caller's session transaction, so the reservation and a content save commit together or not at all. A hold can expire (`ReleaseExpiredAsync` returns its units), and holds are confirmed or released explicitly. Every receipt, reservation, confirmation, release and expiry is a movement, and the balance is their sum. Not gapless, and not `INumberAllocator`. New tables `barako_stock_counters`, `barako_stock_holds` and `barako_stock_movements` (`migrations/5.2.0/580-stock-reservations.sql`). No HTTP surface yet.
