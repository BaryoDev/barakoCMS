- **A service principal needs content rights the caller holds.** Granting a role to a principal checked the role's capabilities but not its content type rights, so a key steward with narrow content rights could hand a principal wider ones and use them through its key. The caller must now hold each enabled create, read, update, delete and transition right on each type the roles carry, with the same conditions (or none) and with field lists that cover the granted ones. Disabling a principal and replacing its roles also need the caller to hold what the principal holds. The same check now applies when adding or updating a tenant member and when assigning a role to a user. Only roles newly added to a member need to be held; keeping, dropping and suspending do not. The seeded tenant Admin role counts as holding every content right in its tenant for this check, but only for roles that tenant owns; capabilities are still checked exactly, and global or platform roles get the full check.
- **Global roles and group roles with rights are SuperAdmin only.** A global role reaches every tenant, so a hold in one tenant proves nothing. Assigning a global role that carries a capability or content right to a user, and creating, updating, restoring, or adding a user to a user group whose roles carry any, now needs a SuperAdmin. Roles with no rights are unaffected.
