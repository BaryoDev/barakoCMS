- **A service principal needs content rights the caller holds.** Granting a role to a principal checked the role's capabilities but not its content type rights, so a key steward with narrow content rights could hand a principal wider ones and use them through its key. The caller must now hold each enabled create, read, update, delete and transition right on each type the roles carry, with the same conditions (or none) and with field lists that cover the granted ones. Disabling a principal and replacing its roles also need the caller to hold what the principal holds. The same check now applies when adding or updating a tenant member and when assigning a role to a user, and changing a member needs the caller to hold the roles the member has now. The seeded tenant Admin role counts as holding every content right in its tenant for this check; capabilities are still checked exactly.
