Fields an owner has not listed are removed from list, read, history and by-slug responses for callers without view_sensitive, and are refused in field filters and left out of search, including a search across all types. History uses the owner's current choice for every version. Anonymous delivery, public search text (also after a sensitivity change), lookups, block bindings, forms, AI indexing, query connectors and the ledger never serve them. A config or bundle import that starts hiding a field rebuilds the search text of the type's stored entries. Adding ShownWhenListedIn to a field through AddField does not need a rebuild, since the field is new and has no stored values.
