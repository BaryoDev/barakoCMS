- **`GET /api/portability/export` and `POST /api/portability/import` are removed, as #1384 promised.** Use `GET /api/config/export`, `POST /api/config/plan` and `POST /api/config/apply`, which carry content types and entries (the `contents` section) in one document. `GET /api/config/export` takes an optional `types` list, such as `?types=article,author`, that narrows `contentTypes` and `contents` to those types and leaves other sections whole. A blank `types`, more than 100 names, or a name over 200 characters answers 400. Entries are filtered in the database, so a tenant over `Portability:MaxImportRecords` can export one type at a time. The old bundle's `contentTypes` and `contents` arrays are read as they were, with `version` set to 1. A role that imported needs `manage_config` as well as `import_content` and `manage_content_types`. It needs no `export_content`. `IConfigProvider` gains the default member `PlanShowsStoredValues`, false for the `contents` section, so a plan of it takes `export_content` or `import_content` and an apply takes `import_content`. `ConfigCaller` gains `ExportTypes` and `WithExportTypes`, which carry the `types` list to a section. `PortabilityBundle` and `ImportRequest` are gone, `ContentRecord` and `ImportReport` are no longer public, and `PortabilityLimits.MaxImportContentTypes` is removed. The Portability module's HTTP contract goes from 3 to 4, the core API contract stays at 17, and `BarakoCMS.Portability` goes to 5.0.0.
