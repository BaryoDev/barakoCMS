- **A form can have an opening and closing date.** `PUT /api/forms/{contentType}` takes optional `windowContentType`, `opensAtField` and `closesAtField`, naming date fields of a singleton content type. A submission outside the window is refused with 403, and editing the date moves the window with no restart. The anonymous form definition reports `open` and `closedMessage` only when a window is set. A form with no window behaves as before.
