- **Two accounts could hold the same email or username in different cases.** The unique indexes
  were on the value as entered and every lookup compared the lowercased one, so two registrations
  for `A@example.com` and `a@example.com` running at the same time could both insert, and every
  sign-in by email scanned the whole users table. `User` now stores `NormalizedUsername` and
  `NormalizedEmail`, the unique indexes are on those, and the lookups read them. Sign-in by username
  ignores case as a result. An existing database needs `migrations/4.2.0/user-normalized-identity.sql`,
  which refuses and names the accounts if two already collide. (#638)
