- **The key list says what a rotation linked and what kind each key is, and the role list says how many people hold each role.** `GET /api/api-keys` items carry `replacedBy` and `replaces` (set by a rotation from now on, null for keys rotated earlier and for keys never rotated, which a client reads as not known) and `kind` (`user`, `servicePrincipal` or `readOnly`, derived from what is stored, so existing keys answer with no migration). `GET /api/roles` items carry `holderCount`, the people holding the role in its tenant scope directly or through a group, read with one statement for the page and returned only to a caller who may list users or manage roles. `ApiKey` gained two public nullable properties, `ReplacedByKeyId` and `ReplacesKeyId`, which old documents load without. No key hash or secret is returned, no contract number moves, and rotation behaviour is unchanged.
