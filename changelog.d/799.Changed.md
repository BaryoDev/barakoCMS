- **Postgres in `docker-compose.prod.yml` and the quickstart is sized for a 2 GB server and records
  query statistics.** `shared_buffers` 512MB, `effective_cache_size` 1GB, `random_page_cost` 1.1 and
  `jit` off, each overridable with a `PG_*` variable; `max_connections` and `work_mem` keep their
  stock values. `pg_stat_statements` is preloaded, and a one-shot `postgres-extensions` service
  creates it on every start, so an existing volume gets it too. The next `up -d` restarts Postgres
  once. `docs/deploy-in-production.md` lists the values for 2, 4 and 8 GB and the stock ones.
  `scripts/delivery-load.py` runs a fixed-rate load against public delivery and reports p50, p95
  and errors.
