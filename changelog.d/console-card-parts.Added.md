- **Console cards can carry visual parts.** `ConsoleCardContent` has optional `Stats`, `Series`, `Bars`, `Checks`, `RowsLabel` and `Question` properties, with records for each. They are init properties, so the constructor is unchanged, and a part left null is not written to the JSON. A console that does not know them ignores them. `MODULES.md` lists the caps and the question box contract.
