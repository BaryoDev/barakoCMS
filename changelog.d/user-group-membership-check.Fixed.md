- **Adding or removing a user from a group now stays inside the caller's tenant, and removal keeps the role limits that adding has.** This covers `POST /api/users/{userId}/groups`, `DELETE /api/users/{userId}/groups/{groupId}` and `DELETE /api/user-groups/{groupId}/users/{userId}`. A user outside the tenant answers 404 and is left unchanged. A caller without `manage_user_membership` in a global role can no longer remove members from a group that holds any role, and only a SuperAdmin can remove members from a group that holds a platform role. Both answer 403. `apiContractVersion` is now 14.
