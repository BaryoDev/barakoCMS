- **A station key can no longer carry a platform capability.** Creating a station with
  `manage_tenants`, `manage_roles`, `manage_platform_roles`, `manage_users`, `manage_email_settings`,
  `view_hidden` or `*` is refused with a 400 naming the capability, the same set member role grants
  refuse. Station keys that already hold one are not revoked, so check `GET /api/stations` for them
  and revoke by hand. `ApiContract.Version` goes from 9 to 10.
