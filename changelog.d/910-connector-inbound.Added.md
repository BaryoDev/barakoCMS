- **One connector describes a party in both directions.** A connector takes an optional `inbound` section (`scheme` of `Hmac` or `Svix`, `allowedEvents`, and a write only `secret`). The secret is encrypted under `Connectors:Key` and stored as `InboundSecret`, and no read returns it: the response has `inbound.secretSet`. A connector stored before this has no section and works as before. The `Webhook` workflow action takes a `Connector` parameter in place of `Url` and `Secret`: the connector's base URL is the target and its `SigningSecret` signs the delivery. A workflow that sets `Connector` with `Url` or `Secret` is refused on save, and a connector that is missing, disabled or another tenant's fails the action with no request. See `docs/connectors.md` (#910).
