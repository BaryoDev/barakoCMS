- **QR codes for a field's value.** New module `BarakoCMS.Qr` serves `GET /api/public/{type}/{id}/qr/{field}?format=png|svg&size=8`. A field is encoded only when it is listed under `Modules:Qr:Fields:<type>` (empty by default), the entry is published and public, and the field is Public on its type. Anything else is a 404. Values over 1024 characters are refused, and `size` is capped at 20 pixels per module.
