- **Accounting reconcile can compare a journal against the sum of several fields.** `GET /api/accounting/reconcile` read the subject amount from `Total` only, so a type that posts Amount plus Fee showed every entry as a mismatch. It takes an optional `amountFields` value, comma separated, and sums those fields. It defaults to `Total`, so existing calls behave as before. At most 8 fields are allowed after blanks and repeats are dropped, and more is a 400. Every named field must be public on the type, as `Total` already had to be. A named field an entry does not hold counts as zero.
