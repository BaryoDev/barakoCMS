- **Check workflows with a schedule `InState` before you upgrade.** Two schedule bugs are fixed in this release (see Fixed). Until now a schedule matched no entry when its `InState` differed from the entry's state only in capital letters, or when it named the type's initial state and the entry had never moved. Such a schedule did nothing and reported no error. After the upgrade it runs its actions, such as sending mail or moving entries, on the entries in that state whose dates fall in its window. Before upgrading a live site, list the workflows with a schedule trigger that sets `InState` and confirm each one should run.
