- **A tenant can choose which external sign-in providers it offers.** `PUT /api/tenants/external-providers` sets the list, `GET` and `DELETE` read and remove it, gated like the role map. A provider the tenant leaves out cannot be started or called back for it, and `GET /api/auth/providers?club={handle}` reports only what it allows. A tenant with no list uses the deployment's providers. Credentials stay deployment-level. New table `migrations/5.5.0/915-external-auth-provider-settings.sql`.
