- **Grant named fields to one connector.** A connector can list `allowedFields` as `Type.Field`, and its Request steps, dry run, collection syncs and `{{query.*}}` rows may then send those Sensitive or Members fields. Nothing else changes: a Hidden field cannot be granted, other connectors, webhooks, Publish, Email and SMS keep sending Public fields as before. Saving a grant needs `manage_connectors` and read on each field added, and only a `manage_connectors` holder sees it. Moving a connector's `baseUrl` clears it. Every run reads the field's level again. A workflow save is refused for a saver who cannot read a granted field its templates name, the dry run masks a value the caller cannot read, and a header holding a granted value is stored redacted. Removing or renaming a granted field, or deleting its type, answers 409 until the grant is taken out.
