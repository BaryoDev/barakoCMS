- **Workflows can create an entry and move one through its lifecycle.** The `CreateEntry` action creates an entry of a content type from `Data.<field>` parameters, validated like `POST /api/contents`, and returns its `Id` as an output. The `Transition` action runs a named lifecycle transition on the triggering entry as the system actor `workflow`. A refused create or move fails the step. Both appear in `GET /api/workflows/actions`.
