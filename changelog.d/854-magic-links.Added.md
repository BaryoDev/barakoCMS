- **Sign in with an emailed link.** `POST /api/auth/magic-link/request` with `{ "email": "..." }` emails a single-use link valid for 15 minutes and always answers 200, whether or not the address is registered. `GET /api/auth/magic-link/confirm?token=...` only shows a confirm button, so mail scanners cannot spend the link. `POST /api/auth/magic-link/confirm` with `{ "token": "..." }` spends it and returns the same tokens as `/api/auth/otp/verify`, including the MFA challenge when the account has MFA. A tenant that disallows `otp` sign-in disallows links too. `MagicLinkToken` is a new document type.
