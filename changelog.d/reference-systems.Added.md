- **Two more built-in blueprints, `inventory` and `attendance`.** `inventory` has products with an `OnHand` sum and a `MovesThisMonth` count kept by rollups over stock moves, and sales orders whose `Total` must equal their lines. `attendance` has employees and time entries, with `Hours` computed from `TimeIn` and `TimeOut` and `HoursLast7Days` and `EntriesThisMonth` kept by rollups. Both are internal, with no slug and no public delivery. Apply them with `POST /api/content-types/blueprints/{name}`; `docs/blueprints.md` lists the shapes.
