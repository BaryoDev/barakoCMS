- **A saved query can group, count and sum in the database.** `POST /api/queries` takes optional `groupBy` (a field, with a `day`, `month` or `year` bucket for dates), `measures` (`sum`, `count`, `avg`, `min`, `max`, each with a name, and an optional `via` reference field for one hop) and `range` (a date field with a relative window or `from` and `to`). `POST /api/queries/{slug}/run` returns the groups and totals, with `from` and `to` in the body to move the range. Figures are computed under the caller's own field and row rules: a field they may not read refuses the run by name, hidden rows are not counted, and days follow the site's time zone. More than 1000 groups is refused. Preview returns the same rows. A query without the new keys behaves as before.
- Saving a query with measures now refuses `fields`, `sortField` and `limit`, and `range` or `groupBy` without a measure. A date with no zone is read in the site's time zone, and a malformed stored date is left out instead of failing the run.
