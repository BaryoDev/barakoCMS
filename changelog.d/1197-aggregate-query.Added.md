- **A saved query can group, count and sum in the database.** `POST /api/queries` takes optional `groupBy` (a field, with a `day`, `week` (ISO week, labelled by its Monday), `month` or `year` bucket for dates), `measures` (`sum`, `count`, `avg`, `min`, `max`, each with a name, and an optional `via` reference field for one hop) and `range` (a date field with a relative window such as `last7days`, matched without regard to case, or `from` and `to`). `POST /api/queries/{slug}/run` returns the groups and totals, with `from` and `to` in the body to move the range. Figures are computed under the caller's own field and row rules: a field they may not read refuses the run by name, hidden rows are not counted, and days follow the site's time zone. More than 1000 groups, or a query that runs past 30 seconds, is refused with a reason. Preview returns the same rows. A query without the new keys behaves as before.
- Saving a query with measures now refuses `fields`, `sortField` and `limit`, `range` or `groupBy` without a measure, and a `count` over a field that is not a number. `from` and `to` at run time need a saved range. A date with no zone is read in the site's time zone, and a malformed stored date is left out instead of failing the run.
