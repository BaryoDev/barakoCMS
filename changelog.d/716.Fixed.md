- **A page could be its own parent, or two pages each other's.** The reference check confirmed the
  target existed and had the right type, and nothing walked the chain, so a renderer following
  `ParentPage` never finished. `ParentReferenceHook` declares a reference field to be a parent:
  register it for a type and field, and an update that points the field at the entry itself, closes
  a loop of any length, or puts the entry more than `MaxDepth` levels deep (64 by default) is
  refused with 400 naming the field. The walk runs inside the write's transaction under an advisory
  lock, so two concurrent saves cannot close a loop between them. Other self-typed references, such
  as a related post pointing back, are untouched, and a type with no hook registered accepts exactly
  what it did before.
