- **A computed field could expose an input that has a role list or a mask.** The definition check only compared sensitivity level, so a computed field readable by more roles than its input, or showing more of its value than the input's mask, was accepted, and raising an input's level, role list or mask afterwards through `PUT /api/content-types/{name}/fields/{field}/sensitivity` was not checked at all. A computed field now has to list only roles its inputs list and be masked at least as strictly, and the sensitivity route refuses with 400 a change that would break that. Raise the computed field first, then the input. A computed field with a role list over a Sensitive or Hidden input that has none is refused too, since the list would let its roles read the value without the capability. Roles are compared exactly, case included. A type stored before this check keeps accepting unrelated changes; its existing computed fields are not checked again until they or their inputs change.
