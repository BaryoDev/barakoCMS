- **`POST /api/preview` is deprecated, and its token is now an entry share link.** The body, the
  status codes and the `?preview=` query on `GET /api/public/{type}/{slug}` are unchanged, so a
  caller needs no change, and a signed-in caller's answer carries a `Deprecation` header. The token used to be a
  signed JWT that could not be revoked and left no record. It is now the key of a 30 minute link:
  stored hashed, listed on the entry with `preview: true`, audited as `site.share_link.created` and
  revocable through `DELETE /api/contents/{id}/share-links/{linkId}`. A JWT issued before the
  upgrade is still accepted until it expires. `ApiContract.Version` does not move (#857).
