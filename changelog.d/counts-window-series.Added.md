- **Content and run counts take a window and a series.** `GET /api/contents/counts` and `GET /api/workflow-runs` accept `range` (`24h`, `7d`, `30d`) or `from` and `to`, and `series` (`hour` or `day`). A windowed answer adds `window`, the counts of the previous window of the same length for a delta, and, with `series`, one bucket per hour or day. A window is at most 90 days and a series at most 200 buckets, 400 past either. Without the parameters the responses are unchanged.
