- **Every commit that changes delivered content now purges the CDN for tenants that turned purge on.** The purge is a durable `PendingCdnPurge` row written after the commit and sent by a background sender, with a debounce window, retries with backoff and a cap on tags per flush. A failed purge never fails the write. Settings are under `Delivery:CdnPurge`, and the migration is `migrations/5.9.0/561-pending-cdn-purges.sql`. See `docs/cdn-purge.md`.
