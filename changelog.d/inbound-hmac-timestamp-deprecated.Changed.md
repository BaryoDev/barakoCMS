- **A plain HMAC inbound webhook with no timestamp header is deprecated, and is refused in 6.0.** `InboundWebhookRequest` with the `Hmac` scheme and no `TimestampHeader` still verifies the body alone, with no replay window, and the verifier now logs a warning once per provider saying 6.0 will refuse it. Name a `TimestampHeader` to sign `{timestamp}.{body}` inside the five minute window. A sender that cannot send a timestamp sets the new `AllowBodyOnlySignature = true`, which keeps the body-only check in 6.0 and stops the warning. Svix is unchanged.
