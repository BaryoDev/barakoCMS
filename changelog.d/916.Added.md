- **Creating or changing a role, and creating or revoking an API key, left nothing in the audit log.**
  `role.created` and `role.updated` are new actions, and the update row holds the capability lists
  before and after with what was added and removed. `apikey.created` and `apikey.revoked` are new
  too, and a row holds the key's id, name, scopes and the user it acts as, never the key, its hash or
  its prefix. Creating a tenant now records its creator as the first member in the new tenant's log.
  Existing rows say more: `role.deleted` carries the capabilities the role held,
  `tenant.member.updated` and `tenant.member.removed` carry the status and roles held before,
  `user.role.assigned` and `user.role.removed` carry the role's name, a sensitivity change carries
  the role list and mask before and after, and `contenttype.field_added` carries the new field's
  level and role list. Each row is saved with the change it describes. The table is in
  `docs/access-control.md`. Reads of Sensitive fields are not recorded yet (#916).
