- **`POST /api/tenants/{handle}/domains/{domain}/verify` moves a pending domain to verified.** It checks a CNAME to the deployment host (`Tenancy:DeploymentHost`, else the host of `App:BaseUrl`) and a TXT record at `_barako-challenge.{domain}` holding the claim's token. Limited by the new `domain-verify` rate limit policy. A tenant domain can no longer be the deployment's own host or a subdomain of it, and names with non-ASCII letters are stored in ASCII form. Adds the `DnsClient` package (Apache-2.0).
